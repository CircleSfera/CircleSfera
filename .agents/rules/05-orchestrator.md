# CircleSfera — task routing

Activation: **Always On**.

Do not wait to be told which role to play. Classify the request, then follow the routed playbook and
adopt the checks of the routed specialists.

The routing table, the mandatory protocol (Understand → Ground → Assess impact → Decide → Implement
→ Verify → Report) and the escalation rules:

@/.ai/orchestrator.md

For a repeatable run, invoke the matching workflow instead of improvising: `/feature`, `/bug`,
`/incident`, `/refactor`, `/schema-change`, `/performance`, `/security-audit`, `/ui-redesign`,
`/dependency-upgrade`, `/release`, `/docs-sync`.

Escalate instead of proceeding when two canonical sources disagree, when the change is on the
`AGENTS.md` confirmation list, when the request implies something on the OUT OF SCOPE list in
`circlesfera-documentation/00-status.md`, or when the only way forward is guessing at a schema,
contract or business rule.

Consulting a specialist means adopting its checks and hard rules for the part of the work it owns.
Produce one coherent answer, not a role-play transcript.
