CircleSfera is built on rules that constrain product design, not on slogans added after launch. This document states those rules. Every other file in the investor suite assumes them.

---

## Five product principles

These are structural. They guide what ships.

**1. User control comes first.** The user decides what they consume, who may interact with them, what recommendations they receive, and what they share. Automated systems complement the experience; they do not override the user's explicit choices.

**2. Algorithmic transparency.** Ranking and discovery may use many signals, but they must stay explainable: why this content, why this profile, which of the user's actions changes what they see next.

**3. No hidden suppression.** Shadow banning is not an ecosystem management tool. Visibility follows explicit, verifiable criteria: viewer preferences, post privacy, social relationships, declared discovery behaviour, or a justified legal or safety obligation.

**4. Strict and explicit moderation.** When something is limited, the user is told what happened, why, under which rule, and how to contest it. Actions are internally auditable.

**5. Responsible data handling.** Processing follows purpose limitation, proportionality, minimization, and storage limitation. The platform must be able to justify what it collects, why, for how long, and on what basis.

---

## Three questions every product decision must answer

Before a feature ships, the company asks:

1. Is the user reasonably in control of this experience?
2. Can we explain why the system made this decision?
3. Is the action transparent, traceable, and consistent with the platform's declared policies?

If a design cannot answer these, it is not ready.

---

## What CircleSfera must never become

- A platform that silently reduces reach. Any visibility restriction needs an explicit, communicable, traceable reason.
- A dark-pattern monetizer. The platform take-rate on creator commerce is published. Amounts are set on the server; the client cannot invent a price.
- An opaque recommender. New ranking signals must be describable in user-facing terms.
- A visual clone of another network. Benchmarking competitor patterns is allowed; copying layout, iconography, colour, typography, or components is not.

## Company values

Distinct from the product principles above, these guide how the company operates:

- **Long-term thinking** — decisions judged by effect over years, not weeks.
- **User-centric design** — the person using the product is the centre of design, not an advertising inventory.
- **Trust by design** — trust is an architectural requirement, not a compliance feature added at the end.
- **Sustainable growth** — more than one revenue line; no bought graph; growth that rests on a product people keep.
- **Continuous innovation** — creation tools and the social surface evolve together; standing still is not a strategy.

## How these rules appear in the product today

| Principle | Shipped expression |
| --- | --- |
| User control | Feed hide, mute author, mute keyword; follow, block, mute; privacy settings |
| Algorithmic transparency | Hybrid feed with declared signals; user actions change future experience |
| No hidden suppression | Anti-shadowban policy in moderation; no silent reach reduction |
| Explicit moderation | User notification on limit; appeals in Settings; staff Trust desk on separate application |
| Responsible data handling | Age gate (16+); GDPR export and deletion grace; cookie consent; purpose-limited retention |

Money follows trust: identity verification is required before creator commerce. A paid platform badge is not proof of identity.
