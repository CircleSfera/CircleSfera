## What we believe

CircleSfera is built on five product principles:

1. **User control comes first** — the user decides what they consume, who interacts with them, what they are recommended, and what they share.
2. **Algorithmic transparency** — why this content, why this profile, which of my actions changes what I see.
3. **No hidden suppression** — no shadow ban; visibility follows preferences, privacy, relationships, declared discovery, or a stated legal or safety duty.
4. **Strict and explicit moderation** — what happened, why, which rule, how to appeal; internally auditable.
5. **Responsible data handling** — purpose, proportionality, minimization, retention; the platform can justify what, why, how long, on what basis.

The next durable social company will be judged on trust. These rules are not marketing. They are how the product is built.

## What CircleSfera is

CircleSfera is a technology company whose first product is a multi-format social network. People publish posts, short video, and stories. They edit in an in-browser studio, go live, message privately, follow, and discover. Creators who complete identity verification can sell subscriptions, unlock content, receive tips and live gifts, and promote posts inside the feed. Users can subscribe to the platform itself. Settlement runs through Stripe Connect at an automatic **80 percent to the creator, 20 percent to CircleSfera**.

When the platform limits an account or a piece of content, it says so, and the person can appeal. Staff trust-and-safety work runs on a separate application with its own identities and mandatory multi-factor authentication.

The client is a web application and a progressive web app in English and Spanish. The product is in production. **Public users: none.**

---

## Why now

Payments, identity verification, and live video are ordinary infrastructure. The creator economy is a profession. Users now ask, in public, how ranking and moderation work. Incumbent networks proved they can connect billions of people; they grew by adding layers. A different contract — control, a published fee, a visible trust desk — is still rare.

## Business model (summary)

Three lines, all on Stripe, all optional for the creator:

- **Platform subscriptions** — seed prices €9.99, €19.99, and €49.99 per month; one active plan per user.
- **Creator economy** — 20 percent platform fee on pay-per-view, tips, creator subscriptions, and live gifts.
- **Promotions** — the creator pays CircleSfera for distribution inside the feed.

A five-year planning model exists as a founder forecast. Actuals are zero. See documents 13–15.
