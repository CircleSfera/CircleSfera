## The company

CircleSfera is a technology company. Its first product is a multi-format social network of the same name. That network is how the company meets users and creators, establishes identity, and begins an economy. It is also the base on which later products can be built when the business allows.

Today the company ships one product. Further applications are vision, not a second line in the financial forecast. Architecture, product decisions, and the business model have been written for a firm that will outlive a single application.

---

## Vision

A technology ecosystem in which people build a digital identity, a community, and an income on infrastructure that can evolve.

---

## Mission

Products that make digital relationships more useful: social experiences people can understand, creation tools that sit with the audience, and monetization that does not require a second application.

---

## Purpose

Social platforms stopped being photo albums. They are infrastructure for communication, entertainment, identity, and work. That job needs products designed for trust, for flexibility, and for a creator who expects to be paid where they publish.

---

## Company values

These guide how the company operates. They complement, and do not replace, the five product principles in [00-principles-and-values.md](./00-principles-and-values.md).

- **Long-term thinking** — decisions judged by effect over years, not weeks.
- **User-centric design** — the person using the product is the centre of design.
- **Trust by design** — trust is an architectural requirement, not a compliance feature added at the end.
- **Sustainable growth** — more than one revenue line; no bought graph; growth that rests on a product people keep.
- **Continuous innovation** — creation tools and the social surface evolve together.

---

## Position

CircleSfera is not "a social app." It is a technology company whose first product is a social platform, designed to become the base of a wider digital ecosystem when this network is validated.

The social network is in production. Public users: none. The raise finishes the commercial product, builds new creation tools, and hires engineering, product, and trust. Creators are not paid to post.
