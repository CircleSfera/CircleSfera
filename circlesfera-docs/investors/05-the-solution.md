## One product, five principles applied

CircleSfera is a multi-format social platform. Every major surface maps to a product principle. Before shipping, each feature must answer: Is the user in control? Can we explain the decision? Is the action transparent, traceable, and consistent with declared policy?

---

## User control comes first

People publish permanent posts, short video (Frames), and ephemeral stories with persistent highlights. They follow, comment, collect, and message privately. They search and browse a home feed they can reshape: hide a post, an author, or a keyword, and the system applies it. They block and mute. Privacy and notification preferences are explicit.

Automated discovery complements the experience. It does not replace the user's choices.

---

## Algorithmic transparency

The home feed combines social relationships, interests, and declared discovery behaviour. User actions — hide, mute, follow, engage — change future experience. The platform aims for users to understand why they see a given piece of content and which signals influenced a recommendation.

---

## No hidden suppression

Hidden reach reduction is not a management tool. Visibility follows viewer preferences, post privacy, social relationships, declared discovery behaviour, or a justified legal or safety obligation. When the platform limits an account or a piece of content, it says so.

---

## Strict and explicit moderation

Users can report content and accounts. When moderation applies, the author is notified where appropriate. Appeals live in Settings; outcomes are communicated. Staff trust-and-safety work runs on a separate application with its own identities, role-based access, and mandatory multi-factor authentication. A consumer account cannot open it.

---

## Responsible data handling

Registration requires age 16 or older, enforced on client and server. Cookie consent gates telemetry. Users can export their data and request account deletion with a grace period. Processing follows purpose limitation and retention policies documented in the product compliance materials.

---

## Creation and formats

People create through a composer (posts, Frames, stories) and an in-browser editing studio (timeline editing, export, captions). They go live with real-time video. Diversity of format is a hedge against a single trend.

Native iOS and Android applications are in development. Shells exist; they are not in the stores. The next generation of creation tools is part of this raise.

---

## Money on the platform

Creators who complete identity verification can:

- Sell a creator subscription (VIP)
- Unlock a post, story, or message (pay-per-view)
- Receive a tip
- Receive a live gift during a stream
- Promote a post inside the feed

Users can subscribe to the platform itself (seed prices €9.99, €19.99, and €49.99 per month; one active plan). Settlement is Stripe Connect. The split is **80 percent to the creator, 20 percent to CircleSfera**. Prices are set on the server. The client cannot invent an amount. Identity is verified before money moves. A paid platform badge is not proof of identity.

---

## Client and languages

The client is a web application and a progressive web app, in English and Spanish. Share previews and installability are built in. Invite codes and a three-invite limit per account are already in the product.
