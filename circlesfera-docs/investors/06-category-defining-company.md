## Company, not application

CircleSfera is a technology company. Its first product is a social network. That network is the wedge: it creates the relationship, the identity, and the first economy. Later products can reuse the same identity, payments, and trust infrastructure when this network is validated.

The category CircleSfera defines is **trust-first social** — a platform where user control, algorithmic transparency, no hidden suppression, explicit moderation, and responsible data handling are structural product decisions, not marketing language added after scale.

---

## Why the social network is the wedge

Social is where identity forms, where communities gather, and where creators meet an audience. It is also where trust is won or lost. A company that cannot hold trust on the social surface will not hold it on a second product.

The social network is therefore the first product and the proof point. It is not the ceiling. The long-term design assumes additional products sharing infrastructure. Those products are not in the financial forecast.

---

## Philosophy as a filter

The five product principles and the three questions in [00-principles-and-values.md](./00-principles-and-values.md) are the filter that keeps the product from becoming a pile of trends.

Every feature must answer: Is the user in control? Can we explain the decision? Is the action transparent and consistent with policy? Features that cannot pass are revised before they ship.

This is slower than copying a competitor toggle. It is also why the company is building a category, not chasing a feature checklist.

---

## Several revenue lines by design

Sustainable growth — a company value — means more than one revenue line from the start:

- Platform subscriptions (user pays CircleSfera)
- Creator commerce (fan pays creator; CircleSfera takes 20%)
- Promotions (creator pays CircleSfera for feed distribution)

The business does not depend on advertising alone. Promotions at scale are in development; the billing and feed injection for promotions already ship.

---

## Infrastructure before expansion

Finish this product, then widen. That is deliberate.

Native applications, promotions at volume, the analytical warehouse, and the next creation tools are the work of the current raise — not a second product line. Communities, a business manager, a public developer platform, and compliance certifications remain out of scope until product explicitly reopens them.

---

## What success looks like

A validated social network with real users who create, return, and pay — without the company having bought the graph or violated its own principles to get there.

From that base, the company can add products that inherit identity, payments, and trust. The category is defined by how the first product behaves, not by how many logos appear on a roadmap slide.
