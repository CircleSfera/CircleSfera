## Objective

A social network is worth the activity in it. Growth is not "maximize signups." It is a community that finds value early, then expands — without violating the principles the product is built on.

Five jobs: validate the product · build a committed first community · attract creators who already publish · learn from real use · get ready to scale.

Success is quality of activity, not registration volume.

---

## Product-led growth

The primary growth engine is the product itself. That aligns with user-centric design and sustainable growth — company values that reject a bought graph.

**Already shipped:**

- In-browser creation studio and composer
- Share cards and Open Graph previews
- Progressive web app installability
- Invite links with a three-invite cap per account
- On-platform money (creators do not need an external checkout to earn)

**This raise adds:**

- Native applications in app stores
- Next creation tools worth switching for
- Promotions at volume and analytics to measure what works

People share what they create. Creators stay where creation and money work. The product is the distribution channel.

---

## Who comes first

- People who already live on social apps and want to create and watch in one place.
- Emerging creators (not celebrities) who will try a new channel to grow and get paid.
- Early adopters who give usable feedback.

We do not target celebrities with retainers. Paying people to post contradicts user-centric design and sustainable growth. A stipend rents a week of activity; it does not build a network.

---

## Phased expansion

**Phase 1 — Closed access.** Stability, behaviour, qualitative feedback, UX. Invite gate is in the product. Registration can require an invite code.

**Phase 2 — Controlled expansion.** More people, still compatible with quality. Do not open an empty discovery feed. Every new user should find content worth returning to.

**Phase 3 — Public opening.** Organic content, referrals, and digital marketing only after a stranger can create something in a single session. Creator partnerships mean aligned publishers, not paid posting programmes.
