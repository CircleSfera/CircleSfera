## GTM philosophy

Go-to-market follows the product principles and company values. We do not buy a graph. We do not open before the product is worth a stranger's first session. We do not pay creators to post.

The raise finishes the commercial product, builds new creation tools, and hires engineering, product, and trust.

---

## Launch objective

Launch is not a date. It is a sequence of gates:

1. The product is stable under real use.
2. A stranger can create and publish in one session.
3. Creation tools and on-platform money are worth leaving another app for.
4. Trust operations can handle reports and appeals at the volume we invite.

Until those gates pass, growth stays controlled.

---

## Three phases

### Phase 1 — Closed access

**Goal:** Validate behaviour, stability, and UX with a small cohort.

**Mechanics (already shipped):** Optional invite-code requirement; three invites per account; configurable registration open/closed.

**Success criteria:** Qualitative feedback; no critical trust failures; creation flows complete without support intervention.

### Phase 2 — Controlled expansion

**Goal:** Grow the cohort while maintaining content quality and trust operations capacity.

**Mechanics:** Expand invite allocation; selective outreach to creators who already publish elsewhere; monitor activation and retention weekly.

**What we avoid:** Opening a public For You feed with no content; paying creators to seed activity; marketing spend before conversion is visible.

### Phase 3 — Public opening

**Goal:** Organic and paid distribution after the product earns it.

**Mechanics:** Organic content on other networks; referral loops; digital marketing budget only after phase-two conversion data; PR when there is a real story.

**Creator partnerships:** Aligned publishers who choose to post because tools and economics work — not retainers or stipends.

---

## Why creators would come without being paid

**Creation that is already here:** Composer, editing studio, live streaming.

**Money that is already here:** 80 / 20 split, pay-per-view, tips, creator VIP, live gifts, promotions.

**What this raise adds:** Native apps in stores, next creation tools, promotions at scale, analytics — so publishing on CircleSfera is worth the switch.

Emerging creators need growth and income, not a signing bonus from a platform with no audience.

---

## First markets

Start in few markets. English and Spanish are in the client. Expand internationally only after product and growth indicators hold in the first market.

---

## What we measure when we open

| Category | Metrics |
| --- | --- |
| Acquisition | Signups, verified users, creators who post |
| Activation | Profile complete, first post, first follow, first conversation |
| Retention | D1 / D7 / D30, DAU/MAU, post frequency |
| Monetization | Plan conversion, creators with a payment, GMV, ARPU, MRR |

Until open, every metric is zero. Report them when they exist.

---

## Capital allocation vs GTM

This round does not fund creator stipends or a "buy the graph" campaign. Capital finishes native store submission, scales promotions, provisions the analytical warehouse, builds next creation tools, and hires the team that will operate trust and ship the product.

Marketing line items, if any, belong to phase three and are earned by phase-two evidence.
