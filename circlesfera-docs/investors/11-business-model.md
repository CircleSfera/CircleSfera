## Overview

CircleSfera earns from three lines. All run on Stripe. All are optional for the creator. The model aligns with sustainable growth — more than one revenue line, no dependence on advertising alone, and a published take-rate that creators can inspect before they publish.

If creators earn more, the company earns more.

---

## Line 1: Platform subscriptions

**Who pays:** The user pays CircleSfera.

**What they get:** A platform plan with tiered features. Seed prices are €9.99, €19.99, and €49.99 per month.

**Rules:** One active platform plan per user. After the payment provider's fee, the remainder is the company's.

**Principle mapping:** Monetization is optional and visible. A paid plan badge is not proof of identity.

---

## Line 2: Creator economy

**Who pays:** The fan pays the creator.

**What CircleSfera takes:** 20 percent platform fee on:

- Pay-per-view (post, story, message unlock)
- Tips
- Creator subscriptions (VIP)
- Live gifts

**Rules:**

- Settlement through Stripe Connect Express
- 80 percent to the creator, 20 percent to CircleSfera
- Prices set on the server; the client cannot invent an amount
- Identity verification required before creator commerce
- Creators receive payouts through the Stripe Express dashboard; no in-app withdraw

**Principle mapping:** No dark-pattern monetizer. Published fee. Identity before money.

---

## Line 3: Promotions

**Who pays:** The creator pays CircleSfera.

**What they get:** Distribution inside the home feed. Sponsored posts are marked. Owners cannot burn their own budget. Pause, cancel, and proportional refund on unused budget are supported.

**Status:** Billing and feed injection ship. Operating promotions at volume — pacing, inventory, warehouse reporting — is part of this raise.

**Principle mapping:** Optional monetization; creator chooses spend; transparent sponsored marking.

---

## What the model excludes

- **Advertising as the only line.** Promotions are first-party, creator-initiated, and marked — not a programmatic ad network at scale (that scale-up is in development).
- **Paying creators to post.** Not funded by this raise. Contradicts sustainable growth.
- **Hidden fees.** The 20 percent rate is consistent across commerce types.

---

## Currency and payments

Creator commerce settles in EUR. Webhooks deduplicate events; failed processing returns HTTP 5xx so Stripe retries. Production requires encryption keys and live-video credentials at boot.
