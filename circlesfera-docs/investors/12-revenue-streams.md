## Three families of revenue

All revenue runs through Stripe. All creator-facing monetization is optional. Identity verification is required before money moves on the creator side.

---

## 1. Platform subscriptions

| Element | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Payer | User → CircleSfera |
| Seed pricing | €9.99 / €19.99 / €49.99 per month |
| Constraint | One active plan per user |
| Recognition | Recurring; after payment-provider fee, remainder is company revenue |

Platform plans fund the infrastructure and features available to all users. They are distinct from creator VIP subscriptions.

---

## 2. Creator commerce (20% platform fee)

| Stream | Description | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creator VIP | Monthly subscription to a creator | 20% |
| Pay-per-view | Unlock post, story, or message | 20% |
| Tips | Direct payment | 20% |
| Live gifts | During live stream; catalog prices server-side | 20% |

| Element | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Payer | Fan → creator (via Connect destination charge) |
| Split | 80% creator / 20% CircleSfera |
| Payout | Stripe Connect Express dashboard |
| Gate | Identity verified before commerce |

The fee is applied consistently. Changing it requires coordinated product and engineering work — it is not overridden per feature.

---

## 3. Promotions

| Element | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Payer | Creator → CircleSfera |
| Product | Feed injection (~1 promoted post per 5 organic posts) |
| Marking | Sponsored label on promoted content |
| Controls | Pause, resume, cancel with proportional refund on unused budget |
| Status | Billing ships; scale-up (volume, pacing, reporting) is in development |

Creators pay for reach they choose. The platform does not sell reach the creator cannot inspect.

## Raise use of funds vs revenue

This raise finishes the commercial product, builds new creation tools, and hires engineering, product, and trust. It does not buy creators. Revenue streams activate when users and creators arrive organically after the product is ready to open.
