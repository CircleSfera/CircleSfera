## Philosophy

The founder model uses conservative, measurable assumptions. No revenue from products not in the commercial offer. Growth first; profitability later. Costs reviewed so cash lasts.

The August 2026 raise finishes the commercial product, builds new creation tools, and hires engineering, product, and trust. It does **not** fund paid creator posting. If the original model implied creator stipends, that is not current policy.

## Customer acquisition cost (targets)

| Stage | CAC target |
| --- | --- |
| Closed beta | €0–5 |
| Launch | €12–18 |
| Early scale | €9–14 |
| International | €8–12 |

These combine organic and paid channels. They have not been measured. Do not say "our CAC is €12" in a meeting.

Organic-first GTM (invites, product-led sharing, creation worth switching for) is designed to keep early CAC at the low end. Paid marketing is phase three only.

## LTV / CAC target

**> 3×** across blended cohorts at scale.

At zero users, this ratio is aspirational. It will be measured from the first cohort after controlled opening.

Primary cost drivers: cloud, CDN, storage, payment-provider fees, and people. Creator payouts pass through Connect; the platform fee is revenue, not COGS on creator gross.

## Unit economics and principles

**Sustainable growth** means we do not buy LTV with creator stipends. CAC paid to rent posts destroys the trust brand and produces no durable graph.

**Published take-rate (20%)** means creator unit economics are inspectable before they publish. Hidden fees would inflate apparent LTV and destroy trust.

After the first 90 days of controlled opening, replace targets with measured CAC, LTV, payback period, and cohort retention. Until then, lead every conversation with "targets, not actuals."
