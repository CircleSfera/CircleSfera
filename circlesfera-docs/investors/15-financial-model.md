## Model philosophy

Conservative, measurable assumptions. No revenue from products not in the commercial offer. Growth first; profitability later. Review costs so cash lasts.

The August 2026 raise finishes the commercial product, builds new creation tools, and hires engineering, product, and trust — not creator stipends.
