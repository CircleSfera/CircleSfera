# CircleSfera
## Roadmap

**Your World, Connected.**  
August 2026

---

## Roadmap principle

Finish this product, then widen. The raise finishes the commercial product, builds new creation tools, and hires engineering, product, and trust. Creators are not paid to post.

Roadmap items pass the three product questions: user control, explainability, transparent and policy-consistent action.

---

## Current state (August 2026)

**Shipped:** Multi-format social platform in production — posts, Frames, stories, composer, editing studio, chat, live with gifts, feed preferences, platform plans, creator commerce (VIP, PPV, tips, gifts), promotions (billing + feed injection), trust desk on separate admin application, PWA in English and Spanish, invite rails.

**Not shipped:** Public users. Native apps in stores. Promotions at scale. ClickHouse Cloud. Next creation surfaces beyond composer and studio.

## Phase 2 — New creation tools (this raise)

Build the next creation surfaces on top of the composer and editing studio already in the product. Specific formats are founder-defined raise work — not invented in this document.

Goal: a stranger can create something worth sharing in one session. That is the gate before public opening.

---

## Phase 3 — Controlled opening (after phase 1–2 gates)

| Milestone | Gate |
| --- | --- |
| Closed beta | Invite-only; stability and qualitative feedback |
| Controlled expansion | Cohort growth with content quality |
| Public opening | Organic + earned paid channels; no creator stipends |

See [10-go-to-market.md](./10-go-to-market.md).

---

## Phase 4 — Scale (post-validation)

International markets (evidence-based, not calendar-based). Infrastructure beyond single-host deployment. Revised financial model with measured CAC/LTV.

Items explicitly **out of scope** until product reopens them: communities, B2B Business Manager, public OAuth platform, SSR indexable profiles, subscriber badges, SOC2, public bug bounty.

---

## What this roadmap excludes

- Paying creators to post
- Opening before native apps and next creation tools are ready
- Promising store dates or warehouse dates not set by the founder
- Second product lines in the financial forecast
