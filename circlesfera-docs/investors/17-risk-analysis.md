## Risk framework

Risks are external (market, competition, infrastructure) and internal (opening too early, violating our own principles). The latter are strategic, not secondary.

---

## 1. No public users (central risk)

**Description:** The product has not been used by the public. Retention, monetization, and GTM assumptions are unvalidated.

**Mitigation:** Built production software first. Invite rails and trust operations exist before scale. Open only when a stranger can create in one session. Measure from first cohort; do not quote forecast as actuals.

**Severity:** High until first cohort data exists.

---

## 2. Opening too early

**Description:** Launching before native applications and next creation tools are ready wastes the first impression. An empty discovery feed destroys trust in the product principles.

**Mitigation:** Phased GTM (closed → controlled → public). This raise finishes native apps, creation tools, and team before phase three.

**Severity:** High if ignored.

---

## 3. Violating our own principles

**Description:** Buying creators, silent ranking, or dark-pattern monetization would destroy the trust brand before it forms.

**Mitigation:** Raise explicitly excludes creator stipends. Product principles are structural. Anti-shadowban policy in code. Published 20% fee. Explicit moderation with appeals.

**Severity:** Existential to thesis.

---

## 4. Incumbent copy risk

**Description:** Large networks can copy visible mechanics (mute, published fee) and still hold distribution and graph.

**Mitigation:** Differentiate on contract and execution, not a single toggle. Build a brand that means explicit trust. Accept that copy risk is real; do not claim immunity.

**Severity:** Medium–high ongoing.

---

## 5. Single-host infrastructure

**Description:** Production runs on one VPS with Docker Compose. Adequate for early use; wrong for large scale.

**Mitigation:** Architecture accepts growth (Postgres, Redis, job queues). Scale infrastructure when metrics justify it. Do not claim cloud topology that is not live.

**Severity:** Medium until scale.

---

## 6. Team concentration

**Description:** The last mile is more work than one person can finish. Trust operations, engineering, and product need dedicated people.

**Mitigation:** Raise includes hiring engineering, product, and trust. Partner value beyond capital includes hiring help.

**Severity:** High pre-hire; medium after core team.

---

## 7. Financial model risk

**Description:** Founder forecast assumes growth, conversion, and creator counts that have not occurred. VIP and live gifts are not in the original mix.

**Mitigation:** Label all projections. Operate on base scenario. Rebuild model with shipped monetization lines and measured data after open.

**Severity:** Medium (investor expectation management).

---

## 8. Legal and IP

**Description:** Legal entity, cap table, and IP strategy (MIT licence on application code) need resolution before a priced round.

**Mitigation:** Document gaps in [18-governance.md](./18-governance.md). Founder completes `_internal/founder-inputs.md`.

**Severity:** Medium until priced round.

---

## 9. Regulatory and compliance

**Description:** Social, payments, and data handling face evolving regulation (GDPR, age requirements, payment rules).

**Mitigation:** Shipped: age 16+, export, deletion grace, cookie consent, identity before commerce. SOC2 and public bug bounty are out of scope today.

**Severity:** Medium ongoing.

---

## 10. MIT licence

**Description:** Application code is MIT-licensed. Investors may ask who owns the commercial advantage.

**Mitigation:** Decide relicense or dual structure before priced round. Document in governance.

**Severity:** Low–medium until decision.

---
