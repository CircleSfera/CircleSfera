## Thesis

The next durable social company will be judged on the trust it holds between user, creator, and platform. Incumbents optimized for engagement and advertising scale in an era when users and creators did not ask how ranking, moderation, and money worked. That era is ending.

CircleSfera is a bet that trust, user control, and money that stays on the platform have to be designed in from the beginning — not announced in a blog post after scale.

---

## The five rules

CircleSfera is built on five product principles:

1. **User control comes first** — the user decides what they consume, who interacts with them, what they are recommended, and what they share.
2. **Algorithmic transparency** — why this content, why this profile, which of my actions changes what I see.
3. **No hidden suppression** — no shadow ban; visibility follows preferences, privacy, relationships, declared discovery, or a stated legal or safety duty.
4. **Strict and explicit moderation** — what happened, why, which rule, how to appeal; internally auditable.
5. **Responsible data handling** — purpose, proportionality, minimization, retention; the platform can justify what, why, how long, on what basis.

These are structural. They constrain implementation. They are why the product is different from "Instagram with a fee."

## The company

CircleSfera is a technology company whose first product is a multi-format social network. The network is in production. Public users: none. Recurring revenue: none.

The product already includes posts, short video (Frames), stories and highlights, an in-browser editing studio, private messaging (encrypted at rest), live video with gifts, follows and collections, platform subscriptions, pay-per-view, tips, creator VIP, promoted posts, and Stripe Connect with an automatic 80 / 20 split. Authentication includes passkeys. Trust and safety run on a separate staff application. The client is a web app and a PWA, in English and Spanish.

Still in development: native iOS and Android, promotions at scale, ClickHouse Cloud provisioning, and the next generation of creation tools.

## The problem

Social platforms became the place where people keep an identity, a community, and, for a growing number, a living. They also became difficult to understand. Ranking is opaque. A paid badge is sold as proof of identity. Creators leave the application to get paid. A working creator runs four or five tools to do one job.

The market is large and still growing. The gap is structural, not a missing feature.

---

## Why now

Payments, identity verification, and live video are ordinary infrastructure. The creator economy is a profession. Users ask, in public, how ranking and moderation work. The market is concentrated. Adding features is not a strategy. A different contract is still rare.

---

## Business model

Three lines, all on Stripe, all optional for the creator:

**Platform subscriptions.** Seed prices €9.99, €19.99, and €49.99 per month. One active plan per user.

**Creator economy.** 20 percent platform fee on pay-per-view, tips, creator subscriptions, and live gifts.

**Promotions.** The creator pays CircleSfera for distribution inside the feed.

If creators earn more, the company earns more. The business does not depend on advertising alone. A five-year planning scenario exists as a founder forecast. Actuals are zero.

---

## Go-to-market

Launch is staged. Phase one is closed access: stability, real behaviour, product quality. Phase two is controlled expansion. Phase three is a public opening, with organic distribution, referrals, and paid marketing only after a stranger can create something in a single session.

Invite codes and a three-invite limit are already in the product. Creators are not purchased. They arrive when creation and money work.

This raise finishes the commercial product, builds new creation tools, and hires engineering, product, and trust — not a campaign to buy a graph.

---

## Competition

CircleSfera does not claim share against Instagram, TikTok, or fan platforms. It claims a different starting contract. Large social networks own the graph and keep ranking difficult to inspect. Fan platforms own money and own a thin graph. CircleSfera owns both sides of the job, publishes the take-rate, separates a paid badge from identity, lets the user change the feed, and runs trust as a product surface.

A competitor can copy a setting. Copying a decade of the opposite promise is a different problem. Copy risk remains real. The defence is execution, not immunity.

## Risks

The product has not been used by the public. That is the central risk.

Opening before native applications and the next creation tools are ready would waste the first impression. Incumbents can copy visible mechanics and still hold distribution. Production today runs on a single host, which is adequate for early use and wrong for large scale. The last mile is more work than one person can finish; that is why the raise includes team.

Violating our own principles — opening an empty feed, silent ranking, buying creators — would be a strategic risk equal to any external threat.
