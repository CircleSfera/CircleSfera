# CircleSfera
**Your World, Connected.**

---

## What we believe

CircleSfera is built on five product principles: **user control comes first**; **algorithmic transparency**; **no hidden suppression**; **strict and explicit moderation**; **responsible data handling**. The next durable social company is judged on trust. These rules are structural, not slogans.

---

## The product

A multi-format social network in production: posts, short video, stories, in-browser studio, private messaging, live video with gifts, follows, collections, and a home feed the user can reshape. Creators who verify identity sell subscriptions, unlock content, receive tips and live gifts, and promote posts. Users subscribe to the platform. Stripe Connect settles at **80 / 20**. When we limit an account, we say so; appeals are built in. Trust staff work on a separate application. Web app and PWA in English and Spanish. **No public users.**

---

## Why now

Creators expect to be paid where they publish. Users ask how ranking and moderation work. Incumbents split attention and money across products. CircleSfera was designed as one system with a published fee and a visible trust contract.

---

## Business model

Three lines on Stripe: platform plans (€9.99 / €19.99 / €49.99 per month); creator commerce (20% fee); promotions in the feed. Monetization is optional. Identity before money.

---

## The raise

Capital to **finish the commercial product, build new creation tools, and hire engineering, product, and trust**. Creators are not paid to post. **€2.5M seed** (range €2.5–5M). Native apps, promotions at scale, analytics warehouse, and next creation tools are the work of this round.
