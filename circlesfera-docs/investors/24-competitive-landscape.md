## Category by category

### Large social networks (Instagram, TikTok)

**What they win:** Graph, distribution, creation tools, habit.

**Where the contract breaks:** Ranking opacity; reach management users cannot inspect; creator money often leaves the app (links, external checkout); moderation opaque at scale; paid badges conflated with identity.

**CircleSfera wedge:** Same creation job, different trust contract — control, published commerce rules, explicit moderation, no hidden suppression.

### Fan and membership platforms (Patreon, OnlyFans)

**What they win:** Money, creator loyalty, clear paywall.

**Where the contract breaks:** Thin or absent social graph; audience built elsewhere; not a daily social home.

**CircleSfera wedge:** Graph + money together; fans stay in the social context.

### Real-time / authenticity apps (BeReal-class)

**What they win:** Novelty, short-term engagement spikes.

**Where the contract breaks:** Limited creation and monetization depth; often single-format.

**CircleSfera wedge:** Multi-format platform with studio, live, and commerce — built for retention, not a single mechanic.

## Competitive positioning (one sentence)

CircleSfera competes for **creators and users who want a daily social home with money and trust inside the product** — not for “another feed” and not for “another fan site.”

---

## Copy risk

Incumbents can copy individual mechanics (mute, a fee announcement, a paywall). They cannot quickly copy a decade of opposite promises to billions of users. CircleSfera assumes copy risk is real; defensibility is execution, principles shipped in product, and brand trust over years. See [07-competitive-moat.md](./07-competitive-moat.md).
