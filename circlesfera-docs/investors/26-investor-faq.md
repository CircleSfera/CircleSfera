# CircleSfera
## Investor FAQ

**Your World, Connected.**  
August 2026

Fifteen questions partners ask in first and second meetings. Answers are consistent with the data room; traction numbers are zero unless stated otherwise.

---

### 1. What is CircleSfera in one sentence?

A multi-format social platform where people create, connect, and get paid in one product — built on five principles: user control, algorithmic transparency, no hidden suppression, explicit moderation, and responsible data handling.

---

### 2. Do you have any users?

**No public users.** Production software ships today. Registered public users, MRR, and GMV are **zero**. We built first; this raise finishes the product and hires the team before controlled opening.

---

### 3. Why raise now?

Native apps, next creation tools, promotions at scale, and the analytical warehouse are in development. One founder cannot operate trust, ship mobile, and open a network alone. Capital finishes the commercial product, builds new creation tools, and hires engineering, product, and trust.

---

### 4. How much are you raising?

**€2.5M seed** (target range **€2.5–5M** depending on lead and hiring plan). Instrument and valuation to be set with lead investor. See [18-governance.md](./18-governance.md) and [`_internal/use-of-funds.md`](./_internal/use-of-funds.md).

---

### 5. Why not €3M instead of €5M?

At zero users, €5M requires exceptional justification (pedigree, LOIs, or strategic lead). €2.5M funds 18–24 months and 8–10 hires to reach controlled opening with evidence. We can scale the round if a tier-1 lead underwrites a larger hiring plan.

---

### 6. How do you get users without paying creators?

Product-led growth: creation studio, on-platform money, invites, share cards, PWA. Creators arrive when publishing and earning work — not from stipends. Phased GTM: closed → controlled → public. See [10-go-to-market.md](./10-go-to-market.md).

---

### 7. Why won't Instagram copy you?

They can copy toggles. They cannot copy a business built on the opposite contract for a decade while holding a billion-person graph. Copy risk is real; we compete on execution and trust brand, not immunity.

---

### 8. What is your take-rate?

**20% platform fee** on creator commerce (tips, unlocks, VIP, live gifts). **80% to the creator.** Platform subscriptions and promotions are separate lines. Amounts are server-side; identity verified before money moves.

---

### 9. Why is the code MIT-licensed?

Application code is MIT today. Commercial advantage is execution, brand, data, and the live product — not licence secrecy. IP strategy (relicense, dual structure) will be decided before a priced round. See [18-governance.md](./18-governance.md).

---

### 10. When do you open to the public?

After gates pass: native apps and next creation tools ready; stranger can create in one session; trust desk staffed. Target **M12–M18** post-close for controlled expansion; public opening only with evidence. See [16-roadmap.md](./16-roadmap.md).

---

### 11. What is shipped vs in development?

**Shipped:** Web/PWA social product, commerce, trust desk, appeals, live gifts, studio, promotions billing. **In development:** native store apps, promotions at scale, ClickHouse Cloud, next creation surfaces. See [08-product-overview.md](./08-product-overview.md).

---

### 12. How do you handle moderation and shadowbanning?

No shadowban as policy. Limits are explicit where possible; users can appeal. Staff work on a separate admin application with MFA. See [25-security-and-trust-summary.md](./25-security-and-trust-summary.md) and [19-esg.md](./19-esg.md).

---

### 13. What about GDPR and age?

Age **16+** on registration (client and server). GDPR export and deletion grace ship. Cookie consent gates telemetry. See [25-security-and-trust-summary.md](./25-security-and-trust-summary.md).

---

### 14. Who is on the team?

Shady Feliu, Founder & CEO, built the production platform. First four hires: full stack developer, mobile, trust lead, product designer. See [23-team-and-founders.md](./23-team-and-founders.md).

---

### 15. What do you want from this fund beyond capital?

Hiring help (engineering, trust, product), judgement on **when** to open, and intros to consumer operators who have scaled trust-heavy products. We do not need permission to ship principles — we need scale and people.
