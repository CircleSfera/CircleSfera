## Assumptions

- Fully loaded cost per FTE (EU remote/hybrid): **€70k–€95k/year** (engineering upper band)
- Founder salary in base plan: **€100k/year** (below market; adjust in cap table discussion)
- Infrastructure today: OVH VPS ~**€500–1,500/month**; scales with warehouse and CDN
- No creator acquisition spend in base case until M12+ gate

## Sensitivity

| Change | Runway impact |
| --- | --- |
| +2 engineers at M6 | −3 to −4 months unless raise size increases |
| Trust hire delayed 3 months | Saves ~€25k; **not recommended** — opens moderation risk |
| €500k larger raise | +6 months at same burn or faster hiring without shortening runway |
