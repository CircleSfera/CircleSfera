# Deck outline — 10 slides

> **Deprecated.** Use [_internal/deck-slides.md](./deck-slides.md) for tier-1 copy, speaker notes, and appendix slides.

Build this in Pitch, Keynote, or Gamma. Do not paste the memo onto slides. One idea per slide. No clip-art networks. No fake charts.

**First meeting:** this deck **or** the thesis, not both in the same PDF. a16z often prefers the memo; FirstMark and Lightspeed will take either. Bring the deck if you are on a video call.

---

1. **Title**  
   CircleSfera. *Your World, Connected.*  
   [Founder name] · Raising [amount] · August 2026.

2. **The five principles**  
   User control · algorithmic transparency · no hidden suppression · explicit moderation · responsible data. One line each. This is the thesis slide.

3. **The split**  
   Attention lives in Instagram and TikTok. Money lives in OnlyFans, Patreon, and checkout links. The user should not need two products.

4. **The product (screenshot, not a list)**  
   One real screen: home, a live, or creator earnings. Caption: posts, short video, stories, chat, live, VIP, unlocks, tips, gifts, promotions. Web + PWA live. Native in flight.

5. **How we make money**  
   Three lines only. User → us (plans). Fan → creator (we take 20%). Creator → us (promotions). EUR. Stripe Connect. No invented ARPU.

6. **Stage**  
   Big type: **No public users.** Subline: production software, invite rails built, graph not seeded. This is the raise, not a footnote.

7. **How users arrive**  
   We do not buy creators. Finish the tools; they publish because creation and money work. Invites and shares. Open when a stranger can create in one session.

8. **In flight**  
   Native · ads at scale · ClickHouse Cloud · next creation tools. Done / open. No roadmap wallpaper.

9. **The raise**  
   [Amount]. Three boxes: finish the commercial product · new creation tools · team. No creator stipends.

10. **Ask + team**  
    Who you are and who you hire first. What you want from this firm: hiring, store-vs-web sequencing. Demo link.
