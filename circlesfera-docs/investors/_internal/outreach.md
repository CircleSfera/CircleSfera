# How to send this to a16z, FirstMark, Lightspeed

Process, not a mailing list. These firms do not take a 15-file zip from a stranger.

**Name check.** The firm that belongs next to a16z and FirstMark is **Lightspeed Venture Partners**. Use that name. "Speedlight" is not that firm.

---

## The only sequence that works

1. **Warm intro** to a named partner or principal who owns consumer, social, or marketplaces. Founder of a portfolio company, a lawyer they respect, or an operator they already back. Cold applications and `info@` aliases die in a queue.
2. **Email** ([outreach-email.md](./outreach-email.md)) + **one-pager** ([22-one-pager.md](../22-one-pager.md)). Nothing else.
3. If they reply, send the **thesis** ([20-investment-thesis.md](../20-investment-thesis.md)) and offer a 20-minute walkthrough. Have the video ready.
4. If they ask "how do you get users?", send [10-go-to-market.md](../10-go-to-market.md).
5. If an engineer or CTO-in-residence joins, send [technology.md](./technology.md).
6. Data room (legal, cap table, live numbers) only after they say they are taking it to partners.

Do not send all files on day one. It signals you do not know how they work.

## What each firm is actually for

This is category-fit, not a claim that they will invest.

| Firm | Why CircleSfera is in their mailbox | What they will punish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Andreessen Horowitz** | Consumer + "full stack before launch" is a founder-mode story they understand. They have seen social die from a feature list with no graph. | A memo that hides zero users. A TAM slide. A 40-page technical dump. Emailing the firm instead of a person. |
| **FirstMark** | Consumer DNA (NYC). They have lived through social. A published take-rate and a product-first raise is a conversation they can have. | "We are the next Instagram." Paying creators to fake a graph. No demo. |
| **Lightspeed** | Seed through growth; consumer and marketplace. They will ask when money shows up and who you hire. | Slides that treat native, ads, and warehouse as already live. An ask that is "buy users." |

None of these firms need you to be in San Francisco on day one. They do need English materials, a video that works at 11pm their time, and a founder who can answer "why you" without a script.

---

## The first call (20 minutes)

They will ask five questions. Answer in this order.

1. **What is it?** A trust-first social network with on-platform money. Five principles. 20% fee.
2. **Any users?** No. We built first. That is why we are raising.
3. **How do you get the first 100?** We do not pay them. We finish native and creation so publishing is worth it. Invites and shares do the rest. We open when a stranger can make something in one session.
4. **Why doesn't Instagram just copy the fee and the mute?** They can copy a toggle. They cannot copy a business that has told a billion people the opposite for a decade. Copy-risk is still real; say so.
5. **How much and for what?** €2.5M seed (range €2.5–5M). Finish the commercial product, new creation tools, team. Creators are not paid to post.

If they ask for metrics, the honest sentence is: "Zero. I will send the first weekly sheet when unpaid users start creating."

Do not walk an empty production For You. Walk staging or the video.

---

## What not to do

- Mass-email five partners at the same firm on the same day.
- Put "a16z, FirstMark, Lightspeed" in the subject or in a public tweet.
- Send `.env`, Stripe IDs, or a production admin login.
- Quote a TAM you cannot source.
- Call native shells "we're on the App Store."
- Call the nightly CSV export "we run on ClickHouse Cloud."
- Promise SOC2, a Business Manager, or native store dates you have not set.

---

## After they say no

Ask one question: "Who should see this instead?" Then stop. A clean no from a partner at this tier is useful; a follow-up novel is not.
